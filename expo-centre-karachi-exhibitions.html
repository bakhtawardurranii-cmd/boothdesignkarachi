Build time

How many days do exhibitors get to set up and dismantle?

Across the 18 exhibitions above, the build window is short and the teardown window is shorter.

  • 13 of 18 give three setup days.
  • 4 of 18 give only two, and 1 (TEXPO-27) gives four.
  • All 18 give one day to dismantle.

That is why design should be approved well before move-in. A stand that needs more work than the window allows has to be simplified on the day. Read how stall installation works inside a move-in window, and see how our five-stage process works backward from your setup day. Counts come from the TDAP list.

Timing

When is Expo Centre Karachi busiest?

December 2026 is the busiest month on this list, with 7 exhibitions, and January 2027 follows with 6. Four shows overlap on 24–27 December (International Consumer Products Fair, Beauty Asia, House Hold Asia and Installment Expo). If you exhibit in either month, ask any fabricator about availability when you book space, not when you want to start building.
