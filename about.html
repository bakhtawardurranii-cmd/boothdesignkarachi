About

Design and fabrication, run as one process

Booth Design Karachi designs, fabricates and installs exhibition booths, stalls and kiosks for exhibitors across Karachi. We handle design, fabrication and installation under a single project scope, so you brief the work once rather than coordinating separate vendors for each stage.

How we work

One brief, one process, one deadline

From the first measurement to the final teardown, design, fabrication and installation are managed as one continuous scope of work rather than separate handoffs — the aim is a concept that doesn't need to be re-briefed halfway through.

Who we work with

Exhibitors across Karachi's trade show calendar

We work with brands, agencies and organizations exhibiting at shows across Karachi — from a single shell-scheme stall to a fully custom raw-space build. Whether it's your first exhibition or your twentieth, the process stays the same: a clear brief, a buildable design, and a stand that's ready before doors open.

Proof, honestly

Our portfolio is still being built out

We'd rather tell you that plainly than fill this page with placeholder projects. As real work is completed, it will be added to our portfolio with genuine details — not stock photography standing in for our own projects.
