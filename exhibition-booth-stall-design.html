Service 01 — Design

Exhibition booth & stall design for Karachi exhibitors

The starting point for most exhibitions: a layout, a 3D concept and a branding direction for your exhibition booth or stall — call it expo stall design, expo booth design or exhibition stand design, it's the same job — whether you're in a shell-scheme row or an open raw-space plot.

Start a project Common questions

Quick answer

Exhibition booth and stall design is the layout, 3D concept and branding plan for your exhibition space — covering both shell-scheme fit-outs (working inside an organizer-provided frame) and raw-space stands (designed from an empty floor area). It's the step before fabrication begins.

Who this is for

Built for exhibitors who need a stand that does a specific job

Who it's for

Brands and organizations exhibiting in Karachi

First-time exhibitors who need a stand designed from scratch, and repeat exhibitors refreshing a stall for a new show — in shell-scheme rows or raw-space plots, at any stand size.

Problems it solves

A stand that's designed for your space, not a generic template

Booths designed without real dimensions often don't fit, don't meet height limits, or waste footprint on dead space. This service starts from your actual allocation so the concept is buildable from day one.

Deliverables

Layout, concept and branding — as one design pass

  • Floor plan sized to your allocated stand dimensions
  • 3D concept covering structure, materials and lighting
  • Branding and graphics direction for fascia, walls and counters
  • A design specification handed directly into fabrication planning

Decision factors

Shell scheme design vs. raw space design

Both are exhibition stall design — the difference is what you're starting from.

Shell Scheme

Working within the organizer's frame

Most exhibition organizers provide a basic shell — walls, fascia and sometimes lighting. Design work covers what goes inside and on top of it: counters, graphics, furniture layout and branding, so a standard booth still reads as yours.

Raw Space

Designing from an empty floor

No shell provided — just a marked floor area. The design covers structure, height, walkways and sightlines from scratch. This is where a stand can look genuinely different from the row next to it.

Process

How the design phase runs

01

Brief & measurements

We confirm your exhibition, stand dimensions, budget bracket and objectives — what the booth needs to do, not just look like.

02

Layout & concept

A floor plan and 3D concept covering structure, materials, lighting and circulation within your space.

03

Branding pass

Fascia, wall graphics, counters and signage are worked into the design using your brand assets.

04

Sign-off & handoff

Once approved, the design moves directly into fabrication planning against the same drawing — no re-briefing a separate vendor.
