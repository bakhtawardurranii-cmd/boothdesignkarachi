Two starting points

Shell scheme design vs. raw space design

Both are exhibition stall design — the difference is what you're starting from.

Shell Scheme

Working within the organizer's frame

Most exhibition organizers provide a basic shell — walls, fascia and sometimes lighting. We design what goes inside and on top of it: counters, graphics, furniture layout and branding, so a standard booth still reads as yours.

Raw Space

Designing from an empty floor

No shell provided — just a marked floor area. The design covers structure, height, walkways and sightlines from scratch. This is where a stand can look genuinely different from the row next to it.

Process

How the design phase runs

01

Brief & measurements

We confirm your exhibition, stand dimensions, budget bracket and objectives — what the booth needs to do, not just look like.

02

Layout & concept

A floor plan and 3D concept covering structure, materials, lighting and circulation within your space.

03

Branding pass

04

Sign-off & handoff

Once approved, the design moves straight into fabrication with the same team — nothing gets re-briefed.
