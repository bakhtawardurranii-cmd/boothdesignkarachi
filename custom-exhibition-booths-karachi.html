Karachi Guide — Custom Booths

Custom exhibition booths in Karachi

Some brands need more than a well-designed standard booth — a double-storey structure, an unusual footprint, or a genuinely flagship presence. This guide covers when a custom booth is the right call.

Start a project Common questions

Quick answer

A custom exhibition booth in Karachi is a purpose-engineered structure — often double-storey, irregularly shaped, or built for a flagship brand presence — that goes beyond what a standard modular or shell-scheme booth format can deliver.

When to consider a custom booth

Signs a standard format won't be enough

  • Your allocation is raw space, not a pre-built shell
  • You need a second level or double-storey structure
  • The footprint is irregular or non-rectangular
  • This is a flagship presence where a template stand would undersell the brand

What it involves

More engineering, more approval time

Custom booths need structural engineering for height and load, plus venue or organizer approval before installation — factor both into your timeline from the start, not as an afterthought once the design is already finalised.
