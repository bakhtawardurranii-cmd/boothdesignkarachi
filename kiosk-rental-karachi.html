Before you book

What to decide before you rent a kiosk

  • Kiosk type: food, touch, digital standee, photo booth or custom.
  • Venue and date: the event you are renting for.
  • Space: the floor area you can give the kiosk and the queue around it.
  • Requirements: branding, what the kiosk must hold or show, and anything special.

Size and requirements are what set the price, so settle them first. Checking space before you book also helps; the exhibition booth size guide shows how to plan it to scale.
