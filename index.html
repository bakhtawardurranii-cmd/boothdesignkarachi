Why brands work with us

Design and fabrication under one roof — so nothing gets lost in translation

Most exhibitors lose time and budget coordinating a designer, a fabricator and an installation crew separately. We run design and build as one process, so the 3D concept you approve is exactly what lands on the show floor.

  • One point of contact from concept to teardown
  • Structures built for raw space and shell-scheme rules alike
  • Deadlines planned backward from your exhibition date
  • On-site installation and dismantling handled for you

Local venue knowledge

Built around how Expo Centre Karachi actually works

Expo Centre Karachi guide

Karachi's exhibition calendar runs largely through Expo Centre Karachi in Gulshan-e-Iqbal — from Textile Asia and IGATEX to Build Asia, FoodAg and IDEAS. We plan builds around its hall layouts, load-in windows and shell-scheme rules, whichever show you're exhibiting at.
