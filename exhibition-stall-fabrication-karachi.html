Service 02 — Fabrication

Exhibition stall fabrication in Karachi

Once a design is approved — yours or ours — the structure, panels and graphics are built, then installed on-site before doors open and dismantled once the show closes. This covers exhibition stall fabrication and expo stall fabrication alike.

Start a project Common questions

Quick answer

Exhibition stand fabrication is the workshop build of an approved booth design — structure, panels, counters and graphics — followed by on-site installation before the exhibition opens and dismantling once it closes.

See a recent example of this in practice: our event display and stand fabrication for the Denning 5th Graduation Ceremony at Expo Centre Karachi.

Who this is for

For exhibitors with a design ready to build

Who it's for

Exhibitors with an approved design

Whether the concept came from our design service or your own designer, this covers turning an approved drawing into a physical stand — on schedule for your move-in window.

Problems it solves

Fabrication that hits the move-in deadline

Late-arriving materials or a rushed on-site build are the most common causes of a stand not being ready when doors open. Fabrication is planned backward from your confirmed exhibition dates to avoid that.

What's covered

Three stages of a single scope

Build

Fabrication

Structure, panels, counters and finishes are built against the approved drawing and material spec.

On-site

Installation

Assembled and finished at the venue within the organizer's move-in window, ready before the exhibition opens.

After the show

Dismantling

Structure is taken down and cleared within the move-out deadline, with reusable parts stored on request.

Materials & build quality

Built for one show, or built to last several

Panels, structural framing and finishes are specified against how often the stand will travel — a one-off shell-scheme fit-out is built differently to a modular stand meant to be reused across several exhibitions.

  • Structural framing sized to stand height and load
  • Panel and graphic finishes matched to your brand spec
  • Reusable modular components where the stand will travel
