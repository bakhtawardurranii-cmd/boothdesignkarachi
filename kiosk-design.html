FAQ

Kiosk design — common questions

A kiosk is typically a permanent or semi-permanent retail structure in a mall or commercial space, built for daily use over months or years. An exhibition booth is temporary, built for a specific event and then dismantled.

We design within the technical and visual guidelines that malls typically require, and provide the drawings needed for your approval submission. Final approval remains with the mall management.

Kiosks use more durable, daily-wear materials since they stay in place for extended periods, compared to lighter, faster-build materials used for short-run exhibition stands.

Yes. A kiosk design can be standardized and repeated across locations, with fabrication adjusted for each site's specific footprint and utility connections.
