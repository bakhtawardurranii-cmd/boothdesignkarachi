Service 04 — Kiosks

Kiosk fabrication company in Karachi: design & build for malls and retail

Looking for a kiosk making company in Karachi? Kiosks are a different job from exhibition work: they stay in place for months or years, not days. We design, fabricate and install for daily durability, consistent branding across locations, and mall-approval requirements.

Quick answer

Kiosk design and fabrication covers permanent or semi-permanent retail structures for malls and commercial spaces — built for daily durability over months or years, unlike a temporary exhibition stand.

Who this is for

For retail presence, not one-off events

Who it's for

Brands with a mall or retail footprint

Retailers and brands needing a fixed kiosk presence in a mall, commercial building or retail space — single location or a design meant to repeat across several sites.

Problems it solves

Durability and approval, not just looks

A kiosk built with exhibition-grade materials wears out fast under daily use. This service specifies for daily operation and prepares drawings to the standard mall management typically expects for approval.

Deliverables

Design, build and approval-ready drawings

  • Kiosk design fitted to your allocated mall footprint
  • Durable materials and finishes built for daily operation
  • Drawings prepared for mall-management approval submission
  • Consistent design across multiple locations, if needed

How a kiosk project runs

From mall footprint to handover

01

Brief and site details

You tell us the mall or location, the allocated footprint, what you sell or do from the kiosk, and your brand assets. For a multi-site rollout, we start with one reference location.

02

Design to the mall's guidelines

The layout, counter, display, storage and lighting are designed to fit the footprint and the mall's technical and visual rules, as the mall management gives them to you.

03

Approval drawings

We prepare the drawings for your submission to mall management. Final approval stays with the mall, so the timeline is planned around how long that review takes.

04

Fabrication

The kiosk is built in the workshop with materials specified for daily operation, not the lighter finishes used for a three-day exhibition stand.

05

Installation and handover

On-site installation is scheduled within the working windows the mall allows, including utility connections where the design needs them.

Kiosk formats

Common kiosk formats and what each one needs from the design

Retail kiosk

Product display plus a sales counter

Sells from a walkway. The design balances open product display, a secure counter, lockable storage and branding that reads from both directions of foot traffic.

Service kiosk

Transactions, queues and equipment

Built around a working counter: payment equipment, power and data points, staff storage and enough clearance for a short queue without blocking the walkway.

Promotional kiosk

Campaign-led, brand-first

A branded presence for a product launch or campaign. It still needs mall approval and daily-use durability, so graphics and structure are specified for the full run, not just opening day.

Build considerations

What daily wear changes in the specification

  • Counter tops and edges specified for constant contact, cleaning and impact, not decorative finish alone
  • Framing that holds its alignment after months of leaning, loading and cleaning, rather than panels designed to be taken down after a show
  • Lighting and electrics planned with accessible access points for maintenance, and cable routes kept out of reach of the public
  • Lockable storage sized for stock and cash handling where the kiosk trades
  • Graphics on durable substrates, with a plan for how and when they are replaced

General guidance — the exact specification is confirmed per project against the mall's rules and your operating needs.

Approval

What mall management usually asks to see

Requirements differ between malls, so confirm the list with the mall's leasing or technical team. We work to whatever list they give you.

  • A dimensioned plan and elevations of the kiosk within the allocated footprint
  • A list of materials and finishes, and sign positions
  • Electrical load and socket positions, where the kiosk needs power
  • Fire and safety information, where the mall requires it
  • Contractor details and installation working windows

Sending a quote request? Include the mall name, the footprint dimensions, what the kiosk sells or does, your brand guidelines and your target opening date, then start a kiosk project.

Food kiosks

Food kiosks: aluminium, wood, iron and foldable builds

We build, rent and sell kiosks, including food and beverage kiosks as well as retail kiosks. The material you choose decides weight, durability, look and whether the kiosk can move. These are the four we build in.

Food kiosks: aluminium, wood, iron and foldable builds
MaterialSuitsTrade-off
AluminiumFood and drink counters, kiosks that get moved, semi-outdoor spotsLight and does not rust, and wipes clean easily. It dents more easily than steel and usually costs more than basic iron.
WoodCafés, dessert and mall kiosks where a warm, branded look mattersEasy to shape to a brand. Surfaces near water or heat need a sealed or laminated finish, and it is a poor choice for wet outdoor use.
IronFixed, heavy-duty kiosks with large spans or heavy equipmentVery strong and stable. It is heavy and needs a rust-proof coating to last.
FoldablePop-ups, events and kiosks that must be stored or moved oftenQuick to set up and pack away. The structure is lighter, with fewer fixed features than a permanent build.

Food-specific design points we plan for: counter height and working layout, space for equipment such as fridges, grills and display warmers, the power load, ventilation, and easy-clean surfaces. Malls set their own rules for food kiosks, so we design to the mall's guidelines, as described above. For one-off events, a foldable kiosk can also work beside an exhibition stand.
