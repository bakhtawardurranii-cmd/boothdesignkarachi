Exhibition stand builders in Pakistan: Lahore and Islamabad projects

Exhibiting in Lahore or Islamabad means a different venue and different organiser rules. We offer local services in both cities, so your stand is built and installed where the show is. This page explains how a project runs and what to prepare first.

Quick answer

Booth Design Karachi builds exhibition stands for shows in Lahore and Islamabad as well as Karachi. We offer local services in both cities, and we design, fabricate, install and dismantle as one project, planned backward from your move-in date.

What to plan

Exhibiting in Lahore or Islamabad: what to plan for

Venue rules

Each venue sets its own windows

Move-in and move-out times, hall access and structural limits differ between venues and shows. The organiser's exhibitor manual is the source.

Local service

Built and installed in the city of the show

Our services in Lahore and Islamabad are local, so your stand does not need to be shipped from Karachi.

Installation

Time on site is fixed

There is no spare day to fix mistakes once the move-in window opens, so the stand arrives finished and ready to assemble.

Coordination

One team, one schedule

Design, fabrication and installation sit on a single timeline instead of passing between vendors.

What to have ready

  • The city, venue and hall, plus your stand number
  • Stand dimensions and the height limit
  • Raw space or shell scheme
  • Move-in and move-out dates
  • The organiser's exhibitor manual, if you have it
  • Your logo files and any product you need on display
