Venue guide — Karachi

Planning a booth for a show at Expo Centre Karachi

A planning reference for exhibitors preparing a stand for an exhibition at Expo Centre Karachi — what the venue involves, and the decisions worth making early.

Quick answer

Expo Centre Karachi is a multi-hall exhibition venue in Gulshan-e-Iqbal managed by the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan (TDAP). Before designing a booth for a show there, confirm your space type (raw space or shell scheme), exact footprint and height limit, and the show's move-in/move-out windows.

Practical guidance

Raw space vs. shell scheme at Expo Centre Karachi

Shell scheme

Faster, more budget-controlled

Where the organizer provides a basic frame and fascia, design work focuses on what sits inside it — counters, graphics and branding. Generally quicker to plan and lower cost than a full raw-space build.

Raw space

Full design freedom, more lead time

An open floor allocation with no provided structure. This gives full control over height, layout and form, but needs more planning time and, for larger builds, structural approval before installation.

The venue

What Expo Centre Karachi is

Expo Centre Karachi is a major exhibition venue in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, managed by the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan (TDAP), hosting trade exhibitions across several halls throughout the year — from textile and garment shows to construction, food, pharmaceutical and automotive events. It's the primary venue most Karachi exhibitors will build a booth for.

Before you design

Questions worth answering early

Space type

Raw space or shell scheme?

Confirm which your stand allocation is — this decides whether design starts from an organizer-built frame or an empty floor area, and changes both timeline and budget.

Dimensions

Exact footprint & height limit

Get your confirmed stand dimensions and any hall-specific height restriction from the organizer before a concept is drawn — not after.

Timeline

Move-in and move-out windows

Every show sets a fixed installation window before opening and a dismantling window after closing. Fabrication has to be planned backward from these dates.

Approvals

Structural sign-off, if applicable

Larger or multi-level raw-space builds may need structural approval from the organizer or venue ahead of installation — factor this into your timeline.
