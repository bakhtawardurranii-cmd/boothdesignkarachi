Stall, booth or stand?

Different words, same service

Karachi exhibitors and organisers use "stall", "booth" and "stand" interchangeably, so you will find all three on our pages.

In practice a stall usually means a compact shell-scheme row position and a booth a larger, more open footprint, but the space type matters more than the word. See how stall design and booth design differ or, if your allocation is raw space, start with custom exhibition stands. Kiosks are a separate job: they are permanent retail structures, covered on kiosk design & fabrication, and event kiosks are on kiosk rental in Karachi.

You may also see us described as stall makers, stand builders, booth fabricators or exhibition contractors. They all describe the same work: design, fabrication, installation and dismantling by one team. Exhibitions are our focus, but we also build for brand events and marketing activations. We delivered the event displays for the Denning 5th Graduation Ceremony, and we build photo booths for activations.
