Service 02 — Fabrication

Exhibition stand fabrication, installation & dismantling

Once a design is approved — yours or ours — our workshop builds the structure, panels and graphics, then installs on-site before doors open and dismantles once the show closes.

What's covered

Three stages, one team

Workshop build

Fabrication

Structure, panels, counters and finishes are built in-house against the approved drawing and material spec.

On-site

Installation

Assembled and finished at the venue within the organizer's move-in window, ready before the exhibition opens.

After the show

Dismantling

Structure is taken down and cleared within the move-out deadline, with reusable parts stored on request.

Materials & build quality

Built for one show, or built to last several

Panels, structural framing and finishes are specified against how often the stand will travel — a one-off shell-scheme fit-out is built differently to a modular stand meant to be reused across several exhibitions.

  • Structural framing sized to stand height and load
  • Panel and graphic finishes matched to your brand spec
  • Reusable modular components where the stand will travel
