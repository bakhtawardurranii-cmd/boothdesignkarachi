Planning tips

Where to place the photo booth in a wedding hall

  • Near where guests wait. The entrance foyer or dessert area works well.
  • Away from the stage. A queue should not block the view or the walkway.
  • Close to power. Tell us the nearest point when you book.
  • Room for a queue. Leave space for guests to line up and watch.
