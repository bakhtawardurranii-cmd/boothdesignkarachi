Photo booth price in Karachi: what renting and buying cost

Rent a photo booth for PKR 50,000 to 150,000 per event, or buy one for PKR 500,000 to 1,500,000. This guide shows what moves the price and when buying pays off.

Quick answer

Photo booth rental in Karachi costs PKR 50,000 to PKR 150,000 per event. Buying costs PKR 500,000 to PKR 1,500,000. Buying pays off after about 3 to 30 events, depending on the booth and the rental price you compare it with. Call or WhatsApp 0371 2389089 for your price.

What moves it

What changes the price within each range

  • The booth model. Shadi, wooden and aluminium, branded or digital selfie.
  • Branding. A standard finish or your own design.
  • Your event. Date, venue and how long you need the booth.
  • Custom builds. Quoted from your brief, separate from standard booths.

Break-even

When does buying a photo booth pay off?

Break-even events = purchase price ÷ rental price. Here is the arithmetic for combinations inside our ranges.

Break-even events for buying a photo booth
Purchase priceRental priceBreak-even
PKR 500,000PKR 150,000about 3.3 events
PKR 500,000PKR 100,0005 events
PKR 1,000,000PKR 100,00010 events
PKR 1,500,000PKR 150,00010 events
PKR 1,500,000PKR 50,00030 events

This is simple arithmetic. It leaves out storage, transport, paper and running costs, and the time it takes to run the booth. Rent if you have one or two events. Buy if you have many.
