How we work

From brief to breakdown, in five stages

The same team stays with your project throughout — so what you approve in the concept stage is exactly what stands on the show floor.

01

Brief & space confirmation

We confirm your exhibition, stand dimensions, height limits, space type (raw space or shell scheme) and budget bracket.

02

Concept & 3D design

A floor plan and 3D concept covering layout, structure, materials, lighting and branding direction.

03

Sign-off & specification

Once the concept is approved, we finalize materials, finishes and a build timeline working backward from your move-in date.

04

Fabrication

Structure, panels, counters and graphics are built in-house against the approved drawing.

05

Installation & dismantling

On-site assembly within the venue's move-in window, and dismantling within the move-out window once the show closes.
