Case study — Event Display & Stand Design

Denning 5th Graduation Ceremony — Class of 2026

Event displays and stands designed, fabricated and installed for the Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship's 5th graduation ceremony at Expo Centre Karachi.

Quick answer

Booth Design Karachi designed, fabricated and installed the event displays and stand for the Denning 5th Graduation Ceremony, Class of 2026, at Expo Centre Karachi on 30 August 2026.

Venue

Expo Centre Karachi

Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship

Service

Event Display & Stand Design and Fabrication

The brief

A display presence for the Class of 2026

Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship needed event displays and stands for its 5th graduation ceremony at Expo Centre Karachi — physical structures that carried the "Class of 2026" branding and gave the ceremony a proper stage presence inside the venue, ready before guests and graduates arrived.

The approach

Design, fabrication and on-site installation as one process

We developed the graduation branding into buildable display structures, fabricated them off-site, then transported and installed them at Expo Centre Karachi ahead of the ceremony. As with our exhibition stand projects, the goal was a finished, photograph-ready structure delivered inside the venue's move-in window — not a design that still needed work once it arrived on-site.

What the ceremony needed

On 30 August 2026, the Class of 2026 convocation for Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship was held at Expo Centre Karachi. The ceremony needed a physical presence inside the hall that matched the occasion: branded backdrops for photographs, a display structure themed around the "Class of 2026" milestone, and a stand that guests and graduates would gather around through the evening.

Our involvement: design, fabrication and installation

Our role was the display and stand side of that brief. The "Congratulations, Denning Graduates" messaging, the Class of 2026 branding and the supporting structural elements were developed into a buildable design, fabricated off-site, then transported and installed at Expo Centre Karachi ahead of the ceremony.

On-site work at a venue this size has its own logistics: getting structures into the hall, positioning them within the layout the organisers have set, and finishing the assembly and detailing so everything is presentation-ready before guests arrive. It is the same discipline we apply to exhibition stall installation.

Why presentation matters at a one-night event

Unlike a multi-day trade show, where a stand gets a second look the next morning, a convocation display has a single evening to do its job: hold up under lighting, photograph well and read clearly from a distance. There is no rebuild window if a panel doesn't align or a graphic prints slightly off.

That is the standard we hold ourselves to on a corporate exhibition stand: a structure that looks intentional, is finished properly and is strong enough to stand up to guests moving around and photographing it.

Delivering at Expo Centre Karachi

Expo Centre Karachi is our primary venue focus. A ceremony held there sits within the same move-in and move-out framework as the trade exhibitions we build for: structures sized and finished to work in the hall, installed inside the window the venue and organisers allow, and cleared afterwards without disrupting other bookings. Our Expo Centre Karachi planning guide covers this in more detail for exhibitors preparing their own stands.
