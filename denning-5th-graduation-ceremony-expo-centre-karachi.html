Denning 5th Graduation Ceremony — Class of 2026

Event displays and stands designed, fabricated and installed for the Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship's 5th graduation ceremony at Expo Centre Karachi.

Venue

Expo Centre Karachi

Date

30 August 2026

Client / Event

Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship

Service

Event Display & Stand Design and Fabrication

The brief

A display presence for the Class of 2026

Denning Institute of Technology and Entrepreneurship needed event displays and stands for its 5th graduation ceremony at Expo Centre Karachi — physical structures that carried the "Class of 2026" branding and gave the ceremony a proper stage presence inside the venue, ready before guests and graduates arrived.

The approach

Design, fabrication and on-site installation as one process

We developed the graduation branding into buildable display structures, fabricated them off-site, then transported and installed them at Expo Centre Karachi ahead of the ceremony. As with our exhibition stand projects, the goal was a finished, photograph-ready structure delivered inside the venue's move-in window — not a design that still needed work once it arrived on-site.
