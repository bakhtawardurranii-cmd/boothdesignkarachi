Service 03 — Bespoke Builds

Custom exhibition stands for raw space & complex builds

For exhibitors who need a fully bespoke structure — a custom exhibition stall or custom exhibition booth with an irregular footprint, a double-storey build, or a raw-space plot with no template that fits. This is a different job from standard booth design: more engineering, more approvals, more original structure.

Start a project Common questions

Quick answer

A custom exhibition stand is a purpose-engineered structure — usually for raw space, an irregular footprint, or a double-storey build — where a shell-scheme or modular layout can't meet the brief. It involves more structural design and venue approval than standard booth design.

Who this is for

Not every exhibitor needs a custom build

Who it's for

Raw-space, flagship or oversized stands

Exhibitors with a raw-space allocation, an irregular or oversized footprint, or a flagship presence where a standard modular or shell-scheme format won't achieve the brief.

Problems it solves

Briefs a template can't accommodate

Double-storey structures, non-rectangular plots and fully bespoke brand environments need structural engineering and approval planning that standard stall design doesn't cover — that's the gap this service fills.

If your stand is a standard shell-scheme or modular format, Exhibition Booth & Stall Design is the right starting point instead.

What makes a build "custom"

Engineering decisions a standard stall design doesn't need to make

  • Structural load and height engineering for multi-level or oversized builds
  • Irregular or non-rectangular footprint planning
  • Venue structural approval and safety sign-off coordination
  • Purpose-built materials rather than stock modular components
