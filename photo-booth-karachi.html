Inside an exhibition

Photo booth and stand: plan them as one design

If the photo booth is part of your stand, design them together. A booth added on the day tends to block the entrance, sit under bad lighting or leave no room for a queue. Planned in the layout, it is the first thing visitors see and it leads them to your counter or product display. See exhibition booth and stall design and custom exhibition stands.
