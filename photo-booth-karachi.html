Quick answer

We rent, build and sell photo booths (also written "photobooth") in Karachi for exhibitions, product launches and brand events. A photo booth is an interactive photo station: visitors take pictures and receive or share them, which draws people in and puts your branding in every photo. Photo booth rental costs PKR 5,000 to PKR 40,000 per event, depending on the size and requirements.

Price

How much does a photo booth rental cost in Karachi?

Photo booth rental costs PKR 5,000 to PKR 40,000 per event. The price depends on the size of the booth and your requirements, so a simple setup costs less than a larger booth with more needs.

  • Size: a larger booth needs more space and costs more.
  • Requirements: branding, how visitors receive photos, and anything special for your event.
  • Custom builds and purchases: quoted from your brief, separate from rental.

Why add one

What a photo booth does for a brand

Footfall

It gives people a reason to walk in

Visitors at a busy show pass dozens of stands. A photo booth is an activity, not a display, so it stops people who would otherwise keep walking.

Branding

Your brand appears in every photo

Branded frames, backdrops and signage go home on every phone, which reaches people who never visited your stand.

Dwell time

It keeps visitors at your stand longer

A queue, a pose and a result take minutes, which gives your team time to start a real conversation.

Where it works

Where a photo booth earns its space

  • Exhibition stands. Inside a shell-scheme stall or beside a raw-space build, as the attraction that pulls visitors in.
  • Product launches and brand activations. A standalone branded structure in a mall, lobby or venue.
  • Corporate and institutional events. Ceremonies, conferences and open days, where guests want a branded photo spot.
  • Roadshows and pop-ups. Wherever you need a visible, interactive point on a short deadline.

Before you book

What to decide before you book a photo booth

Most photo booth problems come from decisions made too late. These are the points we settle in the brief.

What to decide before you book a photo booth
DecisionWhat to confirm
Space and footprintThe floor area, the height limit and how much queue space sits around the booth.
FormatEnclosed for privacy, open for speed and groups, or a backdrop-style station for large crowds.
Branding surfacesWhich surfaces carry your logo: frame, backdrop, fascia, floor graphics or props.
Power and lightingWhere the nearest power point is, and whether the venue lighting suits photography.
Visitor flowHow people enter, queue and leave without blocking an aisle or your stand entrance.
Photo deliveryWhether visitors get printed photos, digital copies or both, and how they receive them.
Venue rulesMove-in and move-out windows, and any approval the organiser needs for the structure.

Inside an exhibition

Photo booth and stand: plan them as one design

If the photo booth is part of your stand, design them together. A booth added on the day tends to block the entrance, sit under bad lighting or leave no room for a queue. Planned in the layout, it is the first thing visitors see and it leads them toward your counter or product display. For raw-space builds, the same logic applies at larger scale; see custom exhibition stands.
